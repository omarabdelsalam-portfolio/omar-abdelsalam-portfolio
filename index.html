Strategic approach

How I think

Three principles behind every content decision.

01

Understand the Business and Market

Start with the business objective, the audience, the competitors, the market context, and the problem the brand needs to solve.

02

Define the Direction

Clarify the positioning, the messaging priorities, and the content pillars. Decide what role content should play in supporting the business.

03

Connect Strategy to Execution

Turn the direction into a content plan, scripts, creative concepts, and publishing priorities. Then review performance and adjust.

Strategy doesn't guarantee sales or growth on its own. It gives every piece of content a clear reason and makes results easier to review and improve.

Workflow

From business problem to published content

Content shouldn't start with random posting or isolated creative ideas. It starts with a clear understanding of the business and the audience.

  1. 01

    Business Understanding

    Goals, offer, customers, and what the brand needs content to achieve.

  2. 02

    Market & Audience Research

    Who the audience is, what they care about, and how competitors communicate.

  3. 03

    Positioning & Strategy

    Where the brand should stand, what it should say, and which content pillars support it.

  4. 04

    Content Planning

    Content calendar, formats, and publishing priorities tied to the strategy.

  5. 05

    Creative Execution

    Scripts, captions, design, and video that carry the message.

  6. 06

    Review & Improvement

    Check how the content performed, learn from it, and refine the plan.
